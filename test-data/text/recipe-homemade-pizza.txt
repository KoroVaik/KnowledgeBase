So, Saturday night came around and I decided to make homemade pizza dough. It had been a while since I had whipped up a fresh batch, so I got the ingredients: flour, water, yeast, salt, and olive oil. First, I had to warm up the water to about 110°F, which was easier said than done with my old thermometer. I kept checking the temperature, but it kept dipping. Frustratingly, but I eventually got it right.

Next, I mixed the ingredients in a big mixing bowl. It was a bit messy but the consistency of the dough started to come together. I let it rest for about an hour, then kneaded it a bit more. The dough was still a bit sticky, so I sprinkled a little flour on my hands and kneaded it some more.

By this time, my home oven had preheated to 450°F, which was just right for a good pizza crust. I stretched the dough out on a pizza stone and let it rest for about 20 minutes. I topped it with tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella, and a bit of basil. Then, I slid it into the hot oven and let it bake for about 15 minutes.

The whole process was a bit of a challenge, but it was totally worth it when I bit into that crispy, delicious pizza. The homemade dough had a perfect chew, and the crust was crispy and crunchy. It was definitely a highlight of the evening. Now, I can't wait to try some other pizza recipes and keep experimenting with different toppings.
