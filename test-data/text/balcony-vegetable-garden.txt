Just got back from tending to my little vegetable garden on the balcony. Despite the small container setup and limited sunlight, it’s been surprisingly productive. I started with some seeds in the spring, and now I’m harvesting a mix of lettuce, spinach, and cherry tomatoes. The challenge has been finding the right container size and type that can hold enough soil to keep the plants happy while also fitting on the balcony. I’ve learned that even with the small 5-gallon buckets, I can grow a decent amount of produce. The key has been ensuring the soil is well-drained and has enough nutrients. I’ve also added a small solar-powered light to help boost the growth in the early mornings and evenings when sunlight is a bit sparse. It’s rewarding to see how much you can achieve in a small space with a bit of planning and effort. I can’t wait to see what comes next in my little oasis on the balcony.
