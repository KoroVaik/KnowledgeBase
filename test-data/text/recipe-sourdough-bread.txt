Hey there, it's been a rollercoaster ride in the kitchen this week with my sourdough journey. I started with a big batch of starter that I decided to keep separate from my regular baking. I thought it would be easier to experiment with the sourdough without contaminating my beloved bread recipe. Here are some notes on how it went:

- **Day 1**: I fed the starter with some flour and water, left it to sit at room temperature and checked back in a few hours. It was still active, so I fed it again and left it overnight.

- **Day 2**: I tried to make a sourdough loaf. I followed a basic recipe but was a bit rushed. The dough was a bit too wet, and it wasn't rising as much as I'd hoped. I left it to ferment for 12 hours, but it still felt a bit dense.

- **Day 3**: I decided to make another loaf, this time with a bit more patience. I used a smaller portion of the starter and followed the recipe more carefully. The dough was firmer and rose beautifully after 12 hours of fermentation. I baked it and it came out great – the crust was crispy, and the interior was fluffy and sour. A major win!

- **Day 4**: I made another attempt, but this time I was a bit too eager. I added more flour to the dough, and it didn't rise at all. I left it to ferment for 24 hours, but it was still dense and didn't get a rise at all. That one was a bit of a disaster.

Overall, it's been a learning experience. I'm still working on getting the timing right and the right consistency for the dough. I'm looking forward to more experiments and hoping to nail it soon. Stay tuned!
