Dear Diary,

I sat down at my kitchen table with a cup of coffee, the aroma of freshly brewed beans filling the room, and a stack of recipe books and a notebook in front of me. I had big plans for my weekend, and that plan included making a sourdough starter and then baking a loaf of sourdough bread. It was a weekend I was determined to make my own bread, something I had been trying to do for years but had always put off. And now, finally, it was my turn to embark on this adventure.

I started off by gathering all the necessary ingredients: flour, water, salt, and a clean jar. I had heard from various online tutorials and books that a good sourdough starter needs time to ferment, and that it’s best to let it sit for several days. I decided to take my time and allow the starter to grow slowly and naturally. So, I mixed a small amount of flour and water, covering the jar with a clean kitchen towel and leaving it on my kitchen counter. I gave it a gentle stir every day, making sure to feed it by adding more flour and water to keep it active and healthy. Over the next few days, I watched as the starter began to grow, bubbling and rising in the jar. I was amazed at how quickly it had come to life and how happy I felt at the sight of it.

But then, a few days later, disaster struck. I decided to make my first batch of sourdough bread. I followed a simple recipe I had found online: 1 cup of flour, 1 cup of water, 1/4 teaspoon of salt, and 1 tablespoon of the sourdough starter. I mixed everything together in a bowl, covered it with a towel, and left it to rise for an hour. It seemed to be doing well, but when I checked it after an hour, it had almost doubled in size. I was excited to see how the bread would turn out. So, I followed the recipe and baked it in the oven.

But then, something went wrong. The bread came out of the oven looking perfect. I checked the temperature and made sure it was 375 degrees. I took out the bread, and it was golden brown and looked delicious. But then, as I cut it open, I realized that something had gone terribly wrong. The bread was dense and dry, with a crust that was so hard it was almost inedible. I was devastated. I had been so excited about making my own bread, and now it looked like a complete failure.

I tried to salvage the situation by adding more water and mixing it with the bread, hoping that it would soften and make it more palatable. But it didn’t work. The bread was still dense and dry, and I was left with a loaf that was essentially inedible. I was frustrated and confused. I had followed the recipe exactly, and yet the bread had turned out so poorly. I couldn’t understand what had gone wrong.

I decided to give it another try, this time taking the advice of a local baker who had suggested that I try making the bread in two batches. I followed the recipe again, but this time I made a smaller batch and let it rise for an hour before baking. It seemed to work better this time, and the bread came out of the oven looking much better. It was still a bit dense, but it wasn’t as bad as the first batch. I was relieved that the bread wasn’t completely inedible this time around.

But then, a few days later, I tried making the bread again. This time, I followed the recipe exactly, but the bread still came out dense and dry. I was frustrated. I had tried everything, but the bread just wouldn’t come out right. I was determined to figure out what was going wrong, so I started doing more research. I read about the importance of temperature and humidity, and how they can affect the fermentation process. I learned that the temperature and humidity in my kitchen can vary depending on the season, and that this can have a big impact on the fermentation process. I decided to experiment with these factors to see if it would make a difference.

I started by trying to maintain a consistent temperature in my kitchen. I bought a thermometer and kept an eye on the temperature while I was baking. I also started using a dehumidifier in my kitchen to keep the humidity low. It seemed to help a little bit, but the bread still came out dense and dry.

I was getting desperate. I tried to make the bread with different types of flour, but it didn’t seem to make a difference. I even tried using a different type of water, but it still didn’t work. I was at a loss.

I decided to take a break from making bread for a while and try making some other things instead. I had a lot of other recipes I wanted to try out, and I didn’t want to give up on making bread altogether. So, I focused on making other types of bread, like baguettes and ciabatta. I was still trying to figure out what was going wrong with the sourdough bread.

But then, a few weeks later, I tried making the bread again. This time, I followed the recipe exactly, but the bread came out perfect. It was light and fluffy, with a crust that was just right. I was amazed at how much it had improved, and I couldn’t believe how much better it was than the first batch.

I had finally figured out what was going wrong. It was all about the temperature and humidity in my kitchen. I had been trying to maintain a consistent temperature, but it had been hard to do. And the humidity had been fluctuating too much. It was only when I started using a dehumidifier and keeping an eye on the temperature that the bread had finally turned out perfect.

I was so relieved. I had been trying to make sourdough bread for years, and finally, I had succeeded. It was a huge accomplishment for me, and I felt so proud of myself. I had learned so much about the process of making sourdough bread, and I couldn’t wait to try it out again.

I made a few more batches of sourdough bread, and each time it came out perfect. I was so happy that I had finally figured out what was going wrong, and I couldn’t wait to share my success with others.

I started sharing my sourdough bread with my friends and family, and they were all so impressed. They loved the light and fluffy texture, and the way it tasted. I was so happy that I had finally succeeded in making sourdough bread, and I couldn’t wait to make more batches.

Now, I was looking forward to making more batches of sourdough bread, and I couldn’t wait to see what else I could do with it. I was so excited about the possibilities of making my own bread, and I couldn’t wait to see what else I could come up with.
