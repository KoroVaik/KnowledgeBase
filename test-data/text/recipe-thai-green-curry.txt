Hey there, me again, this time with another recipe note. This time, it's a Thai green curry from scratch. Yum! I found this recipe online and I've been tweaking it to suit my taste. So here's how I do it:

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp curry paste (I use a mix of green curry paste and lemongrass paste to give it some extra flavor)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp palm sugar
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
- 2 cups coconut milk
- 1 large chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp ground red pepper (optional)

Instructions:
1. Heat the coconut oil in a large skillet. Add the onion and garlic and sauté until they are golden brown.
2. Add the turmeric, cumin, coriander, sugar, and curry paste. Stir until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken, broccoli, and tomatoes. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Add the fish sauce, palm sugar, dark soy sauce, and white vinegar. Stir well.
5. Pour in the coconut milk and stir. Bring to a boil and reduce the heat to a simmer. Cook for 10-15 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and the sauce is thickened.
6. Add the lime juice and white pepper. Stir well.
7. Serve over rice and garnish with chopped chives if you have some.

That's it! I hope you enjoy this recipe as much as I do. If you don't have all the ingredients, you can substitute them with something similar. For example, you can use regular curry paste instead of the mix, or use soy sauce instead of dark soy sauce. Enjoy cooking!
