Just watched "Blade Runner 2049" and wow, it's a masterpiece! The visuals are absolutely breathtaking. The cityscapes are vast and futuristic, with a deep sense of desolation and decay. Every frame is a work of art, from the neon-lit streets to the towering skyscrapers. It's as if the city has been built over centuries, with a mix of high-tech and low-tech elements that create a rich tapestry of a world on the brink of collapse.
