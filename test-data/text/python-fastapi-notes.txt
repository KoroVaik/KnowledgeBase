Man, learning FastAPI has been a game-changer. I never thought web development would be so easy and fun. Routing? It’s like setting up your own little playground where you can call functions and routes are your playground equipment. You can create so many routes, and each one is just a function call. It’s like having all your toys at the ready, and you can pick and choose which ones you want to play with.

Pydantic request models? Oh, they’re like having a magical spellbook where you define what your endpoints are expecting. It’s just a matter of writing a few lines of code, and Pydantic does the rest. It ensures that your endpoints get exactly what they need, and if something’s not right, it just doesn’t work. It’s like having a smart friend who knows exactly what you need and only gives you what’s appropriate.

Dependency injection? This is where it really starts to become magical. With Depends, you can get things done without having to pass around a ton of variables. It’s almost like having a group of friends who know what you need and just make it happen. You can define dependencies for your routes, and FastAPI will take care of the rest. It’s just a matter of saying “Hey, I need this,” and the magic happens.

And then there’s the auto-generated docs. They’re like having a personal assistant who knows all about your endpoints and what they do. You can just say “Hey, I want to know what this does,” and it’s like magic; all the details are there. It’s so handy, especially when you’re working with a team or need to share your code with others. It’s just another way FastAPI makes web development a breeze.
