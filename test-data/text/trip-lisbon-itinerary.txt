Hey there, my little travel diary for this weekend in Lisbon! So excited to be exploring this vibrant city. Here's what I've got planned:

Saturday: Start the day with a morning stroll through the charming Alfama neighborhood. It's a bit hilly, but the views of the Tagus River are worth it. Pack a picnic lunch and head to Miradouro de Santa Luzia for the best panoramic view of Lisbon. Afterward, grab a coffee and dessert at Café A Brasileira. It's a tiny, cozy spot with delicious coffee and some great pastries. For dinner, head to Restaurante A Figueira for its traditional Portuguese fare and a good glass of port.

Sunday: After a hearty breakfast at Café de Lisboa, take a tram ride to Chiado, one of Lisbon's most trendy and upscale neighborhoods. Check out the stunning Miradouro da Senhora do Monte and its views of the city. Then, stop at the Lello & Irmão bookstore, a beautiful old building with a stunning interior and great selection of books. For lunch, try Alentejo, a spot with a great view of the city and yummy Portuguese cuisine. In the evening, head to the Bairro Alto Hotel area for some upscale dining and some nightlife.

Monday: Start with a visit to the National Archaeological Museum in the Baixa neighborhood. It's a treasure trove of Roman and Visigoth artifacts. Afterward, head to the famous Bairro Alto area for more upscale dining and drinks. Walk along the Rua Augusto Rosa and check out some of the art galleries. For dinner, try Rua Augusta for its charming atmosphere and great food.

Transportation: Lisbon has a great public transportation system, which makes it easy to get around. The Tram 28 is great for getting around the city. The city also has a metro system, but it's not as extensive as the tram. The best way to navigate the city is on foot though, as many of the most interesting neighborhoods are quite compact. Also, make sure to buy a Lisboa Card, which gives you free or discounted entry to many of the city's top attractions and also includes free or discounted public transportation. 

Stay safe and have a fantastic time in Lisbon! I hope you enjoy every bit of it as much as I am.
