Alright, my Lisbon adventure begins this Friday night! I can't wait to make the most of this long weekend in one of Europe's most charming and vibrant cities. I've got a few days ahead of me, so I'm going to be pretty thorough in my planning, making sure I don't miss a beat in this beautiful city.

### Friday Night
I'm starting off with a walk around the Alfama neighborhood, which is the oldest part of Lisbon and one of the most historic. As I head out of my Airbnb in Chiado, I take the Metro to Praça de Espanha. This station is a bit of a hub and helps me navigate the older parts of town easier. From there, it's a quick walk to the Baixa neighborhood, which is more modern and where the main shopping and eating areas are situated. After my Metro ride, I'll be strolling around Rua da Prata, a pedestrian street that's lined with shops, cafes, and restaurants. I'm planning to have dinner at Restaurante Pousada da Rua, famous for its traditional Portuguese dishes and cozy atmosphere.

After dinner, I'll make my way to my hotel in the Chiado area. I'll be staying at the 4-star Hotel Lisboa, which is conveniently located near many attractions and has its own rooftop terrace. The hotel is perfect for a long weekend, offering both comfort and convenience. As I check in, I'll make sure to grab the hotel's itinerary and any recommendations they have for the weekend. It's already starting to feel like I'm on vacation!

### Saturday Morning
Saturday starts bright and early, with a visit to the famous Mercado da Ribeira. This is Lisbon's oldest and largest market, where I can pick up some fresh produce and local specialties to cook with. After that, I'll head to the Bairro Alto area to explore the charming streets and visit the famous Pasteis de Belem. I'll take the Metro from Baixa to Bairro Alto and walk around the area to find a spot to enjoy the famous custard tarts and pastries. I've read that the best ones are at the original location, which is a bit of a trek, but it's definitely worth the effort.

Following that, I'll head to the Bairro Alto Hotel to check in. This is a 5-star hotel with a great location and a stunning rooftop bar. I'll make sure to spend some time there on Saturday evening and perhaps order some drinks to enjoy the view of the city.

### Saturday Afternoon
Saturday afternoon is for relaxing and exploring the city a bit more. I'll head to the Praca do Comercio, which is Lisbon's main square and a great place to see the city's architecture. From there, I'll make my way to the Museu da Música, which is dedicated to the history and evolution of music. This museum is a bit of a hidden gem and has an extensive collection of musical instruments and historical pieces. If you love music, you'll definitely want to check it out.

Once I'm done with the museum, I'll head to the Alfama neighborhood for a stroll and some lunch. I'm planning to have a traditional Portuguese lunch at Restaurante Dona Paula, which is a cozy restaurant with a charming outdoor seating area. After lunch, I'll continue exploring the neighborhood and visit the iconic St. George's Castle. This is a must-see attraction and offers panoramic views of the city. I'll take the Metro back to my hotel in Bairro Alto and spend the evening exploring the hotel's facilities, perhaps even trying out the rooftop bar.

### Sunday Morning
Sunday is a bit more relaxed, with some time to rest and recharge. I'll start the day with breakfast at my hotel in Bairro Alto and then head to the Bairro Alto Hotel's spa for a relaxing massage. This is a great way to wind down and prepare for the last day of my trip.

After the massage, I'll head to the Bairro Alto area for some shopping and exploring. I'm planning to visit the charming streets and check out the local boutiques and artisan shops. I'll make sure to take some time to explore the local art scene and check out some of the galleries in the area. After that, I'll head to the Chiado neighborhood for some more shopping and exploring. This is a great area to find some unique and affordable souvenirs and gifts.

### Sunday Afternoon
I'll spend the afternoon at the Lisbon Oceanarium. This is a great attraction and one of Lisbon's top tourist destinations. It's a large aquarium that houses over 10,000 marine species and offers a variety of exhibits and shows. The aquarium is particularly impressive, and the view from the top deck is worth the climb. I'll make sure to grab some lunch at the aquarium's restaurant and then make my way back to the hotel.

### Sunday Evening
The evening is for some relaxation and winding down. I'll head back to the hotel and spend the evening unwinding and perhaps having some drinks at the hotel's rooftop bar. This is a great way to end the weekend and reflect on all the amazing things I've experienced in Lisbon.
