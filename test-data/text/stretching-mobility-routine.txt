Hey, me again! I’ve been doing this stretching and mobility routine for weeks now, and it's definitely helping to combat the stiffness that comes with sitting at my desk all day. Here’s what I do every morning:

1. **Neck Circles**: Sitting up straight, I gently rotate my head in a circle 10 times to the left, then 10 times to the right. Helps release any tension in my neck.

2. **Shoulder Shrugs**: I raise my shoulders up to my ears, hold for a few seconds, and then release. Repeat 5 times. This helps relieve any tightness in the shoulders.

3. **Arm Swings**: I swing both arms forward, bending at the elbow, then lower them behind my back. Do this 5 times in each direction. It’s great for loosening the shoulders and upper back.

4. **Palm Pushes**: I place both hands on the desk, palms facing down, and gently push against the desk. This helps stretch the forearms and wrists, which can get really stiff from typing.

5. **Finger Stretches**: I spread my fingers out wide, then bring them together in a tight fist. Repeat 5 times in each direction. This is a great way to loosen up those knuckles and fingers.

6. **Pelvic Tilt**: Sitting up straight, I tuck my pelvis under, then round it out as far as I can. This helps activate the core and get some movement going in the lower back.

7. **Leg Extensions**: If I’m feeling brave, I’ll put my feet together and slowly try to reach down with both hands, trying to touch my toes. This stretches the hamstrings and lower back.

I usually do this right before I start working, and it makes a big difference. The tension seems to melt away, and I feel more energized and ready to tackle my day. Plus, it keeps those aches and pains at bay. Hope this helps someone else out there too!
