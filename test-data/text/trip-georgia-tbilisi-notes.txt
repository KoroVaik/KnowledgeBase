Visited Tbilisi this weekend and it was fantastic! The food scene is amazing—everywhere you turn, there’s a new restaurant or food truck with delicious Georgian dishes. I tried the local khachapuri (cheese pie) and it was heavenly! If you’re into wine, a day trip to the Kakheti wine region is a must. You can taste different types of Georgian wine and even visit a winery where you can take home a bottle. The old town of Tbilisi is also a must-see. The architecture is stunning, and you can find little hidden alleyways with unique shops and cafes. The night falls, and the city comes alive with street performers and a vibrant nightlife scene. Overall, Tbilisi is a delightful city full of culture, food, and hidden gems. Highly recommend!
