Hey, so here’s my little note on my bodyweight home workout that I’ve been doing a few times a week. It’s really simple, no equipment needed, just my body and some space to stretch out. Here’s what it looks like:

Monday: 

- 5 sets of 15 squats 
- 5 sets of 15 push-ups
- 5 sets of 15 lunges

Tuesday:

- 5 sets of 15 mountain climbers
- 5 sets of 15 burpees
- 5 sets of 15 jumping jacks

Wednesday:

- 5 sets of 15 bicycle crunches
- 5 sets of 15 Russian twists
- 5 sets of 15 leg raises

Thursday:

- 5 sets of 15 planks
- 5 sets of 15 side planks
- 5 sets of 15 bird dogs

Friday:

- 5 sets of 15 calf raises
- 5 sets of 15 dead bug exercises
- 5 sets of 15 shoulder shrugs

Saturday:

- 5 sets of 15 side kicks
- 5 sets of 15 Russian twists
- 5 sets of 15 leg raises

Sunday: 

- Rest day or light yoga

I've been sticking to this routine for about a month now and it’s really helping me tone up and build strength. It’s easy to fit into my schedule, and I love the fact that I can do it at home without any special equipment. Plus, I love how it works out my whole body, from my core to my legs and arms. The variety in the exercises keeps things interesting and prevents boredom, which is a big plus for me. Overall, I’m pretty happy with this routine and I’m going to keep sticking to it and see how I can improve it.
