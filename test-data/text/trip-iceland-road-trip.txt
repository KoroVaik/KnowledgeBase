I've been planning this road trip around Iceland's Ring Road for ages. The idea of seeing the stunning landscapes and experiencing the wild beauty of the Northern Lights is alluring. Here’s a quick note to myself:

- **Weather Concerns**: October to April is the best time for Northern Lights, but it can be cold, windy, and snowing. I should check the weather forecast daily. May through September is warmer, but also popular with tourists, which means crowds at popular sites.

- **Must-see Stops**:
  - **Thingvellir National Park**: The famous裂谷，必去。虽然会有游客，但绝对值得一看。
  - **Hverfjall**: This crater lake is a stunning sight and a great spot for a picnic.
  - **Myvatn Nature Baths**: Relax in the warm geothermal baths, a perfect way to unwind after a long day of driving.
  - **Dettifoss Waterfall**: One of the most powerful waterfalls in Europe. It’s a thrilling sight.
  - **Kirkjufell**: A rugged mountain that looks like a fairytale castle. Worth the climb!
  - **Snæfellsjökull National Park**: A must-visit if you want to see a glacier. The landscape is breathtaking.

I need to pack accordingly, especially if I’m planning to see the Northern Lights. I should take extra warm clothes, a sleeping bag, and possibly a portable heater. Also, don't forget to bring a camera and perhaps a telescope to catch a glimpse of the Northern Lights.

I’m excited to start packing and can't wait to hit the road! Let's hope the weather holds out!
